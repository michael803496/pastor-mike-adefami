About Pastor Mike

Pastor Mike Adefami is a devoted servant of God with over 20 years of ministerial experience. His passion for souls and deep commitment to prayer has made him a beacon of hope for many across nations.

He is known for his compassionate counselling, prophetic insight, and unwavering dedication to helping individuals discover their God-given purpose. Whether you're seeking spiritual guidance, healing, or breakthrough, Pastor Mike is here to walk with you.
